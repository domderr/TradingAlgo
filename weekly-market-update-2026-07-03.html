Europe Holds Firmer While Nasdaq Weakness Weighs

The latest weekly dashboard shows a clear split across the monitored markets. European portfolios were generally more constructive, led by Germany40, while US technology exposure showed weaker relative behavior, especially in the Nasdaq100 portfolio.

Germany40 delivered the strongest result of the week, gaining 5.27% against a benchmark advance of 4.13%. Europe50 also remained positive at 1.52%, although it lagged its benchmark at 2.18%. Italy40 rose 1.31%, below the benchmark gain of 3.41%.

The most difficult reading came from Nasdaq100, where the portfolio declined 9.87% while the benchmark rose 0.86%. The portfolio added RKLB and removed WBD, with the hedge unchanged at 12%. USA100 also underperformed, falling 3.42% versus a benchmark gain of 2.17%, while hedge protection increased slightly from 7% to 8%.

UK30 saw the most visible rotation, with AAL.L, STAN.L and VOD.L entering the selection while RIO.L, HSBA.L and BP.L exited. The hedge increased from 12% to 14%, reflecting a more cautious stance after a slightly negative portfolio week.
