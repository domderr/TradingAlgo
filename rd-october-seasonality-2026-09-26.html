October seasonality

How the stocks inside each of the seven Mosaic markets have behaved in October over the last ten years. Work in progress, not a published model.

  • Octobers 2016–2025
  • Stocks 474 across 7 markets
  • Status Research

Short version. October has no reliable edge in these markets. The average October return of the stocks is positive for the S&P 500 (+1.3%) and the Nasdaq 100 (+1.2%), close to zero for Europe, Italy, Germany and Australia, and negative for the FTSE 100 (−0.5%). No market was up in more than 6 of the 10 Octobers. The averages are driven by a few years: 2022 was up in every market, while 2018 and 2023 were down in every market.

How to read this

Method. Prices are daily, adjusted for dividends and splits, from Yahoo Finance in local currency, September 2016 to September 2026. October return = last close of October divided by last close of September, minus one. The market figures are simple averages across the stocks (equal weight); the benchmark ETF of each market is not part of them.

Limits. Members are the current constituents of each index, taken back in time, so the history carries survivorship bias. Some stocks have fewer than ten years of data, and a few tickers could not be downloaded. Ten Octobers is a small sample and includes the 2018 sell-off, 2020 and 2022, so a seasonal pattern cannot be told apart from those episodes with this data.
