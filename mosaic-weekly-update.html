Three portfolios ahead, and two more one-week round trips

After an update in which all seven Mosaic portfolios closed lower, the picture is mixed again: three beat their benchmark and three finished in positive territory. The spread between the best and the worst relative outcome is the widest in a month, from +3.28 points on UK100 to -4.39 points on Nasdaq100.

7 Active markets monitored: USA100, Nasdaq100, Europe50, Italy40, Germany40, UK100 and Australia50.
+3.28 pp UK100 relative spread versus benchmark, the widest of the group and the strongest result of this update.
17.22% Italy40 carries the highest hedge of the group, after rising for two consecutive readings.

UK100 had the strongest result, gaining 2.48% while its benchmark lost 0.80%. The rotation brought BGEO.L back in place of PCT.L, and the hedge barely moved, at 9.01%.

Nasdaq100 was the weakest, down 3.97% against a benchmark up 0.42%. TER returned to the selection after four weeks out, replacing ROST, and the hedge eased to 11.69%. USA100 followed the same direction on a smaller scale, losing 1.80% versus +0.47%.

Europe was quieter. Europe50 lost 1.64% against -0.91% with no rotation, while Germany40 gained 0.52% against a benchmark up 0.88%, its eighth consecutive reading with an unchanged selection. Italy40 declined 0.75% but still beat its benchmark at -1.08%: ENEL.MI replaced ENI.MI and the hedge climbed to 17.22%.

Australia50 closed essentially flat at +0.02% against -0.46%, holding its selection and easing the hedge from 17.28% to 15.55%.

Two rotations restored a holding dropped one reading earlier: GEV on USA100 and BGEO.L on UK100. USA100 now holds exactly the five names it held two readings ago, which means MRK stayed in the portfolio for a single week.
