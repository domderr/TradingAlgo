TradingAlgo Mosaic: selective strength into the new week

The new week opens with a highly selective picture for the TradingAlgo Mosaic portfolios. Dispersion remains high across regions and indices: several portfolios delivered clear relative strength versus their benchmarks, while others were held back by weaker local markets or less favorable sector rotation.

7 Active markets monitored: USA100, Nasdaq100, Europe50, Italy40, Germany40, UK100 and Australia50.

The strongest contribution came from the US portfolios. USA100 performed well, rising 1.28% while its benchmark declined 1.67%. The entry of GEV in place of SLB strengthens a basket already exposed to names such as MU, CAT, FDX and GOOGL.

The Nasdaq100 result was more modest in absolute terms, with the portfolio up 0.13%, but still materially ahead of a benchmark down 3.17%. Rotation was more active here, with MRVL and RKLB entering the selection while WBD and ROST exited. The hedge increased from 10% to 12%, signalling a slightly more cautious stance in a still volatile market.

Europe was more mixed and generally weaker. Europe50 fell 3.68%, underperforming a benchmark down 1.31%, even as the hedge eased from 10% to 9%. UK100 also struggled, losing 3.13% while its benchmark rose 0.92%.

The clear European exception was Germany40, which gained 1.87% versus a benchmark decline of 1.08%. The current selection, including ENR.DE, RWE.DE, IFX.DE, BAYN.DE and DHL.DE, delivered a meaningful weekly spread despite a slightly lower hedge, from 13% to 12%. Italy40 remained weak at -2.54%, broadly in line with its benchmark at -2.72%.

Overall, the weekly message is clear: market dispersion remains elevated, portfolio rotation continues to matter, and hedge management remains central. TradingAlgo Mosaic is now focused on USA100, Nasdaq100, Europe50, Italy40, Germany40, UK100 and Australia50.
