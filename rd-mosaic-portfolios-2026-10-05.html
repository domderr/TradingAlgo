How to read this

Method. Each portfolio holds the five stocks selected by the Mosaic weekly run of 25 September, at equal weight. The portfolio return is the simple average of the five stock returns, in local currency, with no costs, taxes or dividends.

Limits. Seven trading days are far too few to judge a strategy, and the result of one week says little about the next. Prices come from Yahoo Finance. Mosaic entry prices for the European and UK markets were one day behind in the pipeline, so they were replaced with the actual 25 September closes. Two of the thirty-five positions can no longer be traded. These are research notes, not recommendations.
