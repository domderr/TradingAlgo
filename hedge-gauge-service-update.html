TradingAlgo Mosaic Service Update

Introducing the Hedge Gauge

How hedging is managed for Mosaic subscribers, from now on — explained without any technical detail.

Service update

From Hedge Alerts to an Always-On Reading

Since September 2026, hedging is no longer produced automatically as part of the monthly portfolio cycle: it is derived from dynamic monitoring tools and reaches subscribers separately, as conditions change. Until now that meant an email alert whenever the picture shifted. From today, subscribers also have a live view they can check whenever they want, for every one of the seven Mosaic markets.

We call it the Hedge Gauge. It replaces the idea of waiting for an alert with an always-on reading: open it, and see where each market stands right now.

What You'll See

For each of the seven Mosaic markets, the Hedge Gauge shows:

  • A suggested hedge level, from 0% to 100%. Higher means more caution is warranted; lower means less. It is grouped into four plain bands, from limited hedge to crash risk, so the reading is clear at a glance without needing to interpret a raw number.
  • A short recent price history for context, alongside how the suggested level itself has moved over the past few months.
  • A plain-language note on what changed since the last update, so a subscriber checking in after a few days can see immediately whether anything material happened, rather than having to compare numbers themselves.

The Hedge Gauge updates on its own, daily. There is nothing to configure and nothing to calculate — you only need to look.

Access

The Hedge Gauge is reserved for TradingAlgo Mosaic subscribers. It is available from the Mosaic Member Menu inside the Reserved Area, alongside the other member pages.
